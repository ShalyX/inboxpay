CONNECTED INBOX

Gmail connection

Checking connection…

Disconnect removes the Gmail tokens stored by InboxPay and stops future inbox scans across this business's environments. Existing invoices and payment records stay. A scan already in progress may finish.

Disconnecting does not cancel queued payments, turn off autonomy, or revoke the wallet's payment authority. Use Payment settings to manage those separately.

Data export and removal requests

You can also remove the Google grant in your Google Account. InboxPay does not revoke that grant automatically; removing it may affect related access in the same Google project.

What Google lets InboxPay read
Messages, attachments and settings across your mailbox, plus your Google account name and email. InboxPay searches for invoices and reads matching messages and supported PDFs. Those searches don't narrow Google's mailbox permission. This permission doesn't allow sending, editing or deleting mail.
What InboxPay keeps
Encrypted connection tokens, extracted invoice details, source references and payment records in your business workspace.

You can disconnect in Settings → Business details to remove stored Gmail tokens. Existing records, queued payments and payment authority stay; manage automatic payments separately in Payment settings.
